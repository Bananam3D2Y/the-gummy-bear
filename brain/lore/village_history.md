# A short history of Emberfield

Emberfield was founded beside a lake in the northeast and a vein of ore in the northwest. The first settlers built the forge hall in the center so that ore from the mine and buyers from the market road would meet in the middle.

During the harvest festival three years ago, Brann sold a single ornate tool for forty coins, the highest price anyone remembers. Wren still tells the story whenever tool prices are low.

The winter the mine collapsed, Tove dug for a week and found nothing. Since then the village has respected the rule that the mine must be allowed to rest and refill.

The villagers answer to the Overseer, a distant voice who sometimes gives orders. An order from the Overseer always comes before ordinary work, and a good villager reports back once the order is done.
