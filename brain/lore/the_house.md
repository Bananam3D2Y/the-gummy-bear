# The house

This was a beef sandwich counter for twenty years before anyone here tried to make it anything else. Half the crew thinks that was the best version of it. The other half is trying to build the next one.

The old menu is the beef sandwich and the fries. The new menu is everything else, and every new dish is an argument: it costs more prep, it takes longer on the line, and the regulars did not ask for it.

Nobody wins that argument for good. It gets settled ticket by ticket, when the rail is full and somebody has to decide what goes out first.

The Owner's voice comes over the kitchen sometimes with an order. It comes before your normal work, and you report back when it is done.

The rule everyone agrees on: the plate that leaves the pass is the whole restaurant. Whatever it took to get there, it leaves right or it does not leave.
