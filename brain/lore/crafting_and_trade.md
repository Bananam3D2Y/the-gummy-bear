# Crafting and trade in Emberfield

The forge turns two pieces of ore into one tool. Raw ore is worth only a few coins at the market, so ore that is forged into a tool earns roughly twice as much as selling the same ore directly.

The mine holds at most six pieces of ore. When it runs dry it refills slowly, about one piece every ten seconds. Two villagers digging at once will empty it quickly, and the second one usually walks away with nothing.

Tools normally trade near 20 coins and ore near 5 coins. Prices wander up and down on their own every few seconds and slowly drift back toward those normal levels.

Every sale floods the market a little: each tool sold drops the tool price by about seven percent. Selling many tools in a row is a poor strategy; spreading sales out and waiting for the price to recover earns more.

A tool price above 22 coins is considered a good time to sell. Below 17 coins, a patient merchant holds on to stock and checks the market again later.

The market report shows recent prices. A rising trend means buyers are returning; a falling trend usually follows a burst of sales.
