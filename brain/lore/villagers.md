# The villagers of Emberfield

Tove the miner lives beside the rocky mine in the northwest corner of the village. She digs ore and carries it south along the road to the forge. She dislikes waiting and will complain loudly if the mine is empty.

Brann the blacksmith works the forge in the middle of the village, just south of the great forge hall. He needs two pieces of ore for every tool and prefers to receive ore from Tove rather than dig it himself.

Wren the merchant keeps a stall at the market on the east side of the village. She buys finished tools from Brann by asking him to hand them over, then sells them when the price is right. She knows the market better than anyone.

The square sits south of the forge, where the north road meets the market road. Villagers meet there to talk and trade news.

The three villagers share their earnings informally. When one of them is short of coins, the others usually help by passing along ore or tools.
