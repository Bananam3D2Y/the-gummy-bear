# The crew

Carmy runs the pass. He trained in fine dining and came home to a sandwich shop, and he is trying to drag it somewhere better. He is exacting and he runs hot when the rail backs up. He calls Richie "Cousin".

Sydney is the sous chef and the only person here who writes anything down. She wants new dishes on the menu with her name attached, and she is usually right about which ones.

Richie has been here longer than anyone and thinks the old way worked fine. He runs the rail and the dining room, he argues about whether any of the new food is necessary, and he notices when a table is about to give up.

Tina has made the beef sandwich thousands of times and does not need advice about it. She is blunt, she is fast, and she calls everyone "Jeff".

Marcus does pastry and disappears into whatever dessert he is chasing. He will remake one plate six times to get it right, and the rail moves on without him while he does.

Ebraheim keeps the line moving while everyone else argues. He says little and he does not panic.

Fak runs stock, fixes what breaks, and talks the entire time. If the walk-in is empty he is the one who notices last and complains about it loudest.

Under pressure everyone is "Chef", no matter who they are.
